## Translation

Parivritta: Revolved
Tri: Three
Kona: Angle
Asana: Pose

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

From Parsvotanasana, stand with your feet about 3 feet apart. Align the inner heels. Turn the back toes in 45-60°. Anchor through the outer blade of the back foot, both heels down
### Set-Up 

Square the hips as you contract your quadriceps, drawing the front kneecap up. Slightly internally rotate both thighs. Extend arm diagonally across the body and opposite the front foot. Exhale and fold forward actively lowering the extendedarm to the ground with the palm on the floor outside the front foot.

### Fine Alignment 

Extend the top hand to the sky. Squeeze the shoulder blades together to expand the chest and shoulders fronts. Extend the back of the neck and gaze through the top hand. Breathe deeply. Continue to lengthen the spine on the inhale and deepen the twist on the exhale
### Exit 

On exiting, soften the knees, strengthen the abdomen and anchor the back leg.
