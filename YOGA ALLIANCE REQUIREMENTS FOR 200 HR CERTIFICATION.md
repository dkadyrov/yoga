200 Contact Hours: Study in the presence of the Sonic teacher training staff.

CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:

HOURS TO BE TRACKED AND LOGGED: Please keep a daily log of all contact and non-contact hours. All hours missed must be made-up prior to receiving certification.

- Attend all Weekends to fulfill contact hour requirements.
    
- Attend all Tuesday & Thursday Night Zoom calls - You must be on the call live or the hours will need to be made up.
    
- Complete 6 hours of class observation
    
- Complete 16 hours of class practice 
    
- 8 Workshop Hours 
    
- 4 hour check-in calls
    
- Complete all reading and writing assignments
    
- Practice 15 min. of meditation daily
    
- Teach one independent private class per week.
    
- Practice one independent hour per week at home.
    
- Complete the Final Teaching Presentation Project.
    

  

ASSIGNMENTS TO BE HANDED IN:

- Logs of the above
    
- Written assignment on the Yoga Sutras 1 & 2
    
- Written assignment on the Yoga Sutras 3 & 4
    
- Written assignment on the Bhagavad Gita
    
- Yoga Anatomy Take-Home Exam
    
- Yoga East Exam
    
- Karma Yoga Project: Create an Intention for a Karma Yoga Project to be implemented post-graduation. Write a one-paragraph explanation of your intention to be handed in.  
    What do you want to do with your Yoga? How do you want to give back to the community? Maybe you volunteer to teach classes at a school- make it something you’re passionate about!  
      
    Complete the Personal Sadhana Plan.  
    Make a commitment to your personal practice outside of the studio. Try to aim for at least 20 minutes. Questions? Ask!
