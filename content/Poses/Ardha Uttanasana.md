## Translation

Ardha: Half
Ut: Intense
Tana: Stretch
Asana: Pose

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

Stand with the big toes touching or feet hip- width apart. Connect through all four corners of the foot.

### Set-Up 

From [[Uttanasana]], draw the navel in, keep the spine long and strong and the thighs engaged. Lift the upper body parallel to the floor with the hands gently supporting the shins.

### Fine Alignment 

Gently stretch the back of the body. Extend the crown of the head away from the tailbone and stretch the chest open. Soften the ribcage. Breathe deeply into a full and expansive chest. Keep a long neck.

### Exit 

When releasing from the prep into [[Uttanasana]], be conscious to extend from the tailbone, hinging at the hips, and avoid rounding the lower back.
