## Translation

Bhujanga: Serpent/Snake
Asana: Pose
## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

Lie face down on the mat. Place the palms next to the chest with the elbows bent. Press the palms firmly into the floor, spread through the fingers, and gently press the pointer and thumb down. Firm the pelvis and lower abdomen on the floor. Engage tips of feet, shins, and pubic bone into the earth.
### Fine Alignment 

Gently contract the thighs and feel the knee cap rise. Reach through the toes, tops of the feet evenly pressing into the mat. Draw the navel in, firm the buttocks, and keep the spine long. Bring the shoulder blades down the back, and draw them away from the ears. Keep the elbows close to the body. Lift from the sternum and extend the tailbone towards the heels. Spread through the fingertips. Expand the chest and breathe fully into the heart center.
### Exit 

Roll the toes under, draw the navel in, firm the thighs and lift the hips into Downward- Facing Dog, or lie face down on the mat in prostration.
