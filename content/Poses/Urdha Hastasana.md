## Translation

Urdhva: Upward
Hasta: Hand

## Foundation

[[Tadasana]]

## Set-Up

Stand with the big toes touching, heels slightly apart. Connect through all four corners of the foot, and balance the weight evenly between the balls and heels of the feet. Press firmly into the ground.

## Fine Alignment

Same as Tadasana. Retract the shoulder blades, turn the palms open, and gently circle the arms overhead with the palms facing each other, shoulder width apart.

## Exit 

Circle the arms down turning the palms in as you move through the bottom half of the motion
