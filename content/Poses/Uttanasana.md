## Translation

Ut: Intense
Tana: Stretch
Asana: Pose

## Foundation

Stand with the big toes touching or feet hip- width apart. Balance weight evenly between the balls and heels.

## Set-Up

Draw the arches of the feet up, draw the knee caps up and contract the thighs. Draw the navel in and engage the hip flexors. Bring the shoulder blades onto the back and away from the ears. Gaze through legs. Hinge the body at the hips, tip the tailbone up and lengthen the spine. Enter into the fold consciously and soften the knees, if needed.

## Fine Alignment

Reach the crown of the head down. Gently stretch the back of the body. Breathe deeply into a full and expansive chest.

## Exit 

On exiting, soften the knees, strengthen the abdomen and lift with the legs to avoid overexerting the lower back muscles
