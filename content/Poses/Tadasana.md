## Translation

Tad: Mountain
Asana: Pos

## Foundation: 

Stand with the big toes touching, heels slightly apart, or with feet slightly apart. Connect through all four corners of the foot, and balance the weight evenly between the balls and heels of the feet. Press firmly into the ground.

## Alignment

- Set-Up Lift through the arches and inner ankle. 
- Gently contract the muscles of the thigh (quads & hamstrings) and feel the knee cap rise and the knee joint stabilize. 
- Engage the navel center, and keep the spine long and strong. 
- Bring the shoulder blades onto the back, and draw them away from the ears. 
- Extend the back of the neck and neutralize the chin. 
- Gaze straight ahead.

## Exit

Move in and out of this still, meditative standing pose with great awareness and consciousness.
