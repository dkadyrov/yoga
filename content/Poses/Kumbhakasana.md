## Translation

Kumbhak: Breath retention
Asana: Pose

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

Hands are shoulder-width; thumbs in line with the center of the chest, palms are flat. Line the wrist crease up with the front of the mat. Spread the fingers and press gently into the pointer and thumb. Do not collapse the wrists. Firmly press into the floor. The balls of the feet are hip-width apart.

### Set-Up 

Do not ever jump into Plank. Step consciously back from [[Uttanasana]], and be sure that the shoulders are directly over the wrist. If moving forward from [[Poses/Adho Mukha Svanasana]], be sure to keep legs firm, reaching heels back. If the load is too heavy and torso sags, drop knees.

### Fine Alignment 

Lengthen the body from the heels through the crown of the head. Lift from the sternum and extend the tailbone towards the heels. Spread through the fingertips. Soften the rib cage into the torso

### Exit 

Lower the knees gently to the mat, keeping the navel drawn. Keep a slight pike in the hips, preparing for the next posture.
