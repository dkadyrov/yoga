## Translation

Chaturi: 4
- Arms/Elbows
- 
Anga: Limb
Danda: Stick

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

Same as Plank. Hands are shoulder-width; thumbs in line with the center of the chest, palms are flat. Line the wrist crease up with the front of the mat. Spread the fingers and press gently into the pointer and thumb. Do not collapse the wrists. Firmly press into the floor. The balls of the feet are hip-width apart.

### Set-Up 

From Plank, press through heels or gently lower the knees to the mat for the modification. Draw the navel and ribs gently into the body. Draw the elbows in as you lower your chest to hover with elbows at right angles. Shoulders do not drop past the line of the elbow. Engage danda, with crown-head-heels in one line.

### Fine Alignment 

Lengthen the body from the heels through the crown of the head. Lift from the sternum and extend the tailbone towards the heels. Spread through the fingertips. Soften the rib cage into the torso. Expand the chest and avoid dipping shoulders as you lower.

### Exit 

Lower the knees gently to the mat, keeping the navel drawn. Keep a slight pike in the hips, preparing for the next posture.

### Transition

Roll the toes under, draw the navel in, firm the thighs and lift the hips into the air, opening the shoulders into Downward Facing Dog.
