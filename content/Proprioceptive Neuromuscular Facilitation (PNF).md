Advanced stretching and rehabilitation method that combines passive stretching with isometric muscle contractions to rapidly increase range of motion and improve neuromuscular control.

Sensory Stimulation: It targets proprioceptors—such as muscle spindles and Golgi tendon organs—to alter how the nervous system perceives muscle tension and length. 

The Cycle: A muscle is moved to its end range, contracted isometrically against resistance for 5 to 10 seconds, relaxed, and then gently pushed into a deeper passive stretch. 

**Common Techniques:** Methods include 
- Hold-relax
- Contract-relax (CR)
- Contract-relax-antagonist-contract (CRAC)
