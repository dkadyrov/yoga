Sun Salutation
- Roots in ancient practices from [[Bharata]]
- Numerous variations
- Traditionally performed during sunrise
- Moving prayer that activates major muscles and joints
- Can be performed 12 times daily to ensure vital health and spiritual well-being
- Invokes a sense of having reverence or bowing down to the sun with gratitude
- Based upon 12 movements that relate to the solar months, and activates the inner sun
- The names are given in [[Sanskrit]]

Benefits: 
- Each posture has a specific benefit
- Classical Standpoint: Sun salutations are a meditative and dedicative practice
- Gentle Repetition of the Sequence: mind eases its way into a more centered place
- Leaves behind agitations. 

Bio-mechanics and Physiology:
- Moves all major joints of the body through many ranges of motions
- Use muscles in a dynamic, rhythmic fashion
- Lubricate and prepare the joints
- Increase blood flow to the muscles
- Warm the muscles and [[fascia]]
- Increase the heart and respiratory rate to deliver more oxygen to the muscles
- Stimulate the nervous and endocrine systems.
