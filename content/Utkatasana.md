## Translation

Utkata: Fierce
Asana: Pose

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

Stand with the big toes touching or feet hip- width apart. Balance weight evenly between the balls and heels of the feet.
### Set-Up 

Bend the knees and sit back as if sitting in a chair. Keep the knees behind the toes, aligned over the ankles. Lift the arches and inner ankle. Firmly press the knees together. Engage the abdominal and lift the pelvic floor. Keep the spine long. Bring the shoulder blades onto the back, and draw them away from the ears. Turn the palms open and bring the arms overhead with the palms facing each other externally rotating arms.
### Fine Alignment 

Extend the tailbone towards the floor and lengthen the lower back. Reach the arms up, lift through the sternum, opening the chest. Soften the rib cage into the torso. Breathe fully into the chest.
### Exit 

Stand gently, lowering the arms in preparation for the next posture.
