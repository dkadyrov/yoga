## Translation

Vi: In a special away. 
Nyasa: 
- Taq: To Place
- Jeffrey: To Place/To be Arranged in Order

Dynamic style of yoga that links breath with movement. 

Key Elements: Flow, Breath, and Alignment. 

Known as flow yoga due to its smooth transitions between poses. 

Comes from [[Ashtanga Yoga]]
- Vinyasa is a little more creative
- No fixed sequence (not like [[Bikram Yoga]] or [[Ashtanga Yoga]])

Peak Pose themed classes: 
- Prepare body for more advance pose. 
- Start low and slow, building towards 

Breathing: [[Ujjayi Pranayama]]
[[Bandha]]:
[[Drishti]]: Line of Sight. 

Each movement is synchronized with an inhale or exhale. 
- Enhances physical and mental connection
- Improves focus and mindfulness

Breath, Movement, Body Part. 

[[Bhava]]: Energy of the room.

Flow: Seamless sequence of poses.
- Each transition is as important as the pose itself

Alignment: Protects the body from injury and ensures the poses are effective and balanced. 
- Use props if needed to maintain proper form.
- Listen to body and modify as necessary.
- Adaptable to different bodies

Osteopathic Cell Production: Small cells in bones. 

Physical Benefits:
- Builds strength and flexibility
- Improves cardiovascular health
Mental Benefits:
- Enhances mindfulness and concentration
- Reduces stress and anxiety.
