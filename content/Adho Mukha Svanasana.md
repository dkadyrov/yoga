## Translation

Adho: Downward
Mukha: Face
Svana: Dog
Asana: Pose
## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

From [[Kumbhakasana]], press the body into an inverted V shape. Feet are sitting bone width apart. Root the balls of feet and palms. Line the wrist crease with the front edge of the mat. Press the palms firmly into the floor, spreading through the fingers and gently pressing the pointer and thumb down. Do not collapsethe wrists
### Set-Up 

From [[Bhujangasana]], lift the hips back towards the heels, bend the knees, and lift the hips. If the hamstrings are tight, be sure to keep the knees bent, lifting from the navel center and lengthening the spine. From [[Urdhva Mukha Svanasana]], roll over the toes and initiate the lifting of the hips with the strength of the navel center.

### Fine Alignment 

Contract shins and thighs, and draw the knee caps up. Draw the navel in. Rotate the upper arms out and the forearms in. Bring the shoulder blades down the back away from the ears. Gaze through the legs or to the navel. Extend the tailbone, and reach the sitting bones up and the crown of the head down. Engage your thighs and reach the heels towards the floor. Heels should be directly behind the toes, creating a slight inner spiral of the thighs. Breathe deeply into a full and expansive chest.
### Exit 

Soften the knees, draw the navel in and gently step one foot at a time, between the hands as you look forward

## Assistance

- Press into the hands
- Still them, especially in the shoulders
- Press down on the heels to cue bending of the knees
- Press back on the lower back
