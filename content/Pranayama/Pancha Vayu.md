Pancha: 5
Vayu: That which flows

1. Prana vayu: heart, red, right, respiration, autonomic nervous system. Pranic absorption takes place on a large scale in the thoracic regions this is the function of prana vayu. Circulates from the diaphragm to the base of the throat (upward movement). 
2. Apana vayu: anus, white, excretion, elimination takes place largely through the urinary/excretory and reproductive organs and is powered by apana. Vital energy functioning in excretion. Circulates downward from the navel to the feet (downward movement). 
3. Vyana vayu: all-pervading, circulation. Circulation is powered by vyana. All-pervading prana, it circulates in all directions to each cell. 
4. Samana vayu: navel, digestion, milky. In between prana and apana, in the stomach region, is the area of assimilation. circulates from the navel to the diaphragm (center circulation). 
5. Udana vayu: throat, swallowing, white. Movement in the throat and fascial expression are due to udana. vital force near the throat. Circulates from the base of the throat to the top of the head.
