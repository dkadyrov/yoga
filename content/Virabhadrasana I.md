## Translation

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

Lunge Position, pivot the back foot, and drop the heel down to 45°. Align both heels, or allow the feet to be wider. Ground through the outer edge of the back foot. Lift the inner arch, lift the back thigh, and keep the front knee over the ankle

### Set-Up

From a lunge. Bend the front knee so that the shin is perpendicular to the floor and the thigh parallels to the floor.

### Expansion

Draw the front thigh bone into the hip socket and wrap the hip back. Lift the back thigh and straighten the back knee. Engage the abdominal and lift the pelvic floor. Keep the spine long. Reach the arms up, lift through the sternum, opening the chest. Lengthen the back of the neck and neutralize the chin. Breathe into the chest. Avoid twisting the back knee while attempting to square off your hips.

### Fine Alignment 

### Exit 

When releasing the pose, slowly drop the hands to either side of the foot and connect firmly through the palms before stepping back to Plank.
