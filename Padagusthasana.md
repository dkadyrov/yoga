## Translation

Pada = Foot 
Angustha = Big Toe 
Asana = Pose

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

Stand with the big toes touching or feet hip width apart. Balance weightevenly between the balls and heels.

### Set-Up 

Draw the arches of the feet up, draw the knee caps up and contract the thighs. Draw the navel in and engage the hip flexors. Bring the shoulder blades onto the back and away from the ears. Gaze through legs. Hinge the body at the hips, tip the tailbone up, and lengthen the spine. Enter into the fold consciously and soften the knees, if needed. Toe-lock.
### Fine Alignment 

Reach the crown of the head down. Gently stretch the back of the body. Draw the shoulder into the back, away from the ears, and use the arms, not the shoulders, to deepen the forward fold. Breathe deeply into a full and expansive chest.

### Exit 

On exiting, soften the knees, strengthen the abdomen and lift with the legs to avoid overexerting the lower back muscles. Inhale halfway, exhale and place the hands on the hips, inhale all the way up
