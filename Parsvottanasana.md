## Translation

Parsva = Side
Ut = Intense 
Tana = Stretch
Asana = Pose

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

Stand with the feet about three feet apart. Align the inner heels, or separate feet enough for hips to square down. Turn the back toes in 60 degrees, both heels rooted to the earth.
### Set-Up 

Square the hips as you contract your quadriceps, drawing the front knee cap up. Slightly internally rotate both thighs; hands can cometo the floor or blocks. Join the hands behind the back and bring them to prayer or palm-to-elbows or interlace fingers. Draw the shoulder blades together and draw down. Hinge forward at the hip crease, tip the tail bone up, extend the sternum and create length through the spine without rounding the back.

### Fine Alignment 

Consciously square the hips as you lengthen away from them. Squeeze the shoulder blades together to expand the chest and shoulders fronts. Extend the back of the neck and neutralize the chin. Breathe deeply.
### Exit 

On exiting, soften the knees, strengthen the abdomen and anchor the back leg. Slowly raise the torso up.
