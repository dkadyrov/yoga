## Translation
Supta: Reclined
Matsyendra: King of the Fishes
Asana: Pose
## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation
Lie on your back, fully extended, with your head resting on the floor. Deepen the back into the floor
### Set-Up 
Bend Right knee up to chest and cup outside of the knee with the left hand. Shift hips over to the side. Gaze right and draw right shoulder into the back body, rooting the shoulder into the ground.
### Fine Alignment 
Gently move right knee across the body to the left,twisting the torso. Use the left hand to press into the right outer knee, deepening the twist. Make sure the right shoulder remains rooted. Keep the spine long. Breathe deeply
### Exit 
Ease the bent knee back to center, gently draw it in, and then release it back to the floor. Repeat on the other side.
