## Translation

Pavivrtta: Revolved
Parsva: Side
Kona: Angle
Asana: Pose

## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation

See Anjaneyasana – lunge stance.
### Set-Up 

While keeping the knee over the ankle, square the hips as you contract your quadriceps, and lengthen the back leg. Slightly internally rotate both thighs. Draw the navel in and keep the spine long. Bring hands to prayer, fold forward with a straight spine and wrap the opposite elbow around the front knee. Create a straight line from elbow to elbow and wrap the shoulder into the back.

### Fine Alignment 

Press the top hand into the bottom, moving the thumbs toward the sternum, deepening the twist on the exhale. Lengthen the spine and back leg with the inhale. Elongate the crown from the tail. If very open, drop the bottom hand outside the front knee to the floor and extend the top hand to the sky

### Exit 

Exit by placing the palms on either side of the front foot and stepping back into Chaturanga or Downward-Facing Dog.
