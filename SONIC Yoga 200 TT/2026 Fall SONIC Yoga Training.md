## Yoga Sutras Book

- There are 2 written reflections for The Yoga Sutras Book
    
- The first reflection is on books 1 & 2 
    
- The second reflection is on books 3 & 4 
    
- Each are 1 page (it can be longer if necessary) 
    
- Prompt: Write two 1-page reflections on the Yoga Sutras.  You can summarize each book or contrast and compare them.  Alternately you can pick a sutra from each book, and write about it. 
    
- Questions to consider:  What are the main messages from each book. If you picked a sutra or group of sustras, why did you pick them? How does this concept apply to your daily life? To your practice? To your work? Does this compare/contrast with any other readings/teachings? What was the main concept that you took away from the books or sutras?  How can you summarize the message of these teachings?
