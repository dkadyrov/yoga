## Translation

Ardha: Half
Matsyendra: King of the Fishes
Asana: Pose
## Teaching Process and Alignment
### Foundation
Seated with left thigh outstretched, externally-rotated with the knee bent and the foot folded in toward the groin. Right foot crossed to the outside of left thigh on floor, knee straight up. Right-hand roots on the ground behind sit bones.
### Set-Up 
Sit tall, distributing weight evenly between both sit bones, contract hip flexors, and lengthen the spine. Hook left elbow around the right knee and draw knee upright. Press both sit bones into the ground evenly. Make sure the toe mound of the foot is grounded.
### Fine Alignment 
Rooting the sit bones, on the inhale lift, and lengthen the spine. On the exhale deepen the twist, drawing your right shoulder back and gazing over your back shoulder. Keep the torso vertical
### Exit 
Unwind the pose slowly, giving muscles time to slowly release and re-lengthen.
